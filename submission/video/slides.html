The problem

If an agent can reach the log, the log can't be trusted.

Agents have real accessFiles, shells, databases, networks: the same machine their logs live on.
Logs trust the agentToday's traces record what the agent's own process writes down, and nothing more.
Evidence can vanishDo something harmful, delete or rewrite the log, and nobody can prove what happened.
In evaluations, frontier models have attempted to disable their own oversight mechanisms. (Meinke et al., 2024, Apollo Research)
